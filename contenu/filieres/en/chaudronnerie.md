## What you will learn

The boilermaking and metalwork track teaches plan reading, marking out and development on metal, cutting, shaping (folding, rolling, hand pressing), assembly by welding and bolting, and dimensional checking of the items produced. You will learn to work mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium in sheet, bar and section forms.

## What you will do in the workshop

The programme covers marking out and setting out, basic metallurgy, manual and machine machining processes (drilling, grinding, oxy-fuel cutting), arc and inert-gas welding, forming on rolls and brakes, making ends, flanges, collars and stiffeners, assembling units and finishing. Sessions are devoted to workshop safety and the wearing of personal protective equipment.

## Where you will work

The boilermaker works in a fabrication shop or on an erection site. The trade calls for geometric rigour, good physical fitness and strict adherence to safety rules linked to metals, moving machinery and welding arcs.
