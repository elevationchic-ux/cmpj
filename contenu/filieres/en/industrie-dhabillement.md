## What you will learn

The garment manufacturing track covers knowledge of fabrics and textile materials, body measurement, the drafting and construction of basic patterns and their adaptation, the craft of laying patterns on cloth to limit material waste, cutting, assembly and machine making-up, finishing and pressing, and the construction of a complete wardrobe. You will learn to operate the different machines of a workshop (lockstitch, overlocker, coverstitch, industrial iron).

## What you will do in the workshop

The programme includes textile technology, fashion drawing and technical sketching, pattern making (skirts, dresses, trousers, shirts, jackets), cutting and marking, assembly and seams, fitting zips and linings, finishing, professional pressing, garment care and the basics of running a garment-making workshop.

## Where you will work

The garment professional works in a making-up workshop, an alterations shop, a fashion house or on their own account. The trade calls for meticulousness, good visual endurance and rigour on the quality of the finish.
