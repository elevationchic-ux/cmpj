## What you will learn

The electrical installation track covers the fundamental laws of electricity (voltage, current, resistance, power), wiring and connection, cable pulling, fitting accessories (switches, sockets, luminaires, circuit breakers, residual-current devices), reading and producing single-line and multi-line diagrams, bringing installations into compliance, fault-finding and restoration of service. You will be trained in safety rules and first-response actions for electrocution victims.

## What you will do in the workshop

The programme includes basic electrotechnics, single-phase and three-phase alternating-current circuits, accessories and protection, low-voltage distribution, lighting, asynchronous motors, industrial cabinet wiring, extra-low voltage systems (IT, telephone, CCTV), electrical authorisation and prevention of electrical risk.

## Where you will work

The electrician works on construction sites, in maintenance within an industrial or commercial facility, or repairing faults for private customers. The trade demands absolute rigour on the safety of people and property, and the ability to read a diagram and diagnose a fault.
