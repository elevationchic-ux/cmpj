## What you will learn

The car mechanics track covers the operation and rebuilding of the internal-combustion engine (valve train, piston rings, gaskets, cylinder head), the ignition and injection system, the transmission and gearboxes, the running gear, the hydraulic braking circuit, the cooling system, air conditioning, the battery and auxiliary electrical circuits, as well as reading manufacturer wiring diagrams. You will be trained to diagnose and replace worn or faulty components.

## What you will do in the workshop

The programme includes petrol and diesel engine mechanics, injection and fuel systems, manual and automatic gearboxes, axles and transmissions, braking, suspension and steering, cooling and lubrication circuits, the battery, starter motor, alternator and lighting, diagram reading and fault-finding.

## Where you will work

The mechanic works in an independent garage, a dealership, an auto service centre or a transport company. The trade requires good physical fitness, adherence to safety rules with flammable liquids, and a concern for methodical diagnosis before intervention.
