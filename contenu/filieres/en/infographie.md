## What you will learn

The graphic design track trains you in creating and composing visuals on a computer. You will learn colour theory, typography, layout, vector drawing, photographic retouching and compositing, and graphic composition for print and for screen. You will master the main software used in desktop publishing and graphic creation, and be introduced to preparing files for offset or digital printing and to designing visuals for digital media.

## What you will do in the workshop

The programme includes vector drawing (logotypes, illustrations, pictograms, shapes, Bézier paths), photo retouching and compositing (layers, masks, tone curves, colour correction, cut-outs), editorial layout (text, columns, templates, export of print-ready PDF), colour theory and applied colorimetry, typography and visual hierarchies, the basics of poster and advertising-media design, graphic packaging for video, and web-design notions (interface mock-up, web image formats, banner).

## Where you will work

The graphic designer works in an agency, in a company communications department, in a print shop or as a freelancer. The trade calls for creativity, visual culture, rigour with print constraints and the ability to meet a brief and a brand guideline.
