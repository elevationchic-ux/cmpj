## What you will learn

The joinery track covers identifying wood species and wood-based panels (plywood, chipboard, MDF), cutting, machining by machine and by hand tool, jointing (tenon, mortise, halved tenon, biscuit, dowels, glue), making boxes, frames, doors, worktops and furniture. You will learn to read and produce a working drawing, to mark out on wood, to set up machines and to ensure the finish.

## What you will do in the workshop

The programme includes the technology of woods and panels, marking out and cutting, woodworking machines (surface planer, thicknesser, table circular saw, spindle moulder, chain mortiser, pillar drill), traditional and modern joints, making panel doors, glazed doors, windows and simple furniture (bookcase, table, wardrobe), fitting, on-site adjustment and finishing (sanding, varnish, oils).

## Where you will work

The joiner works in a fabrication workshop, on a fitting site or in interior fit-out. The trade demands precision, adherence to dimensions, vigilance around moving machinery and a good ability to carry heavy pieces.
