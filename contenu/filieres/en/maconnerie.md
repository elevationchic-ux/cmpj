## What you will learn

The masonry track covers preparing mortars and concretes, raising structures in brick, block and stone, building load-bearing walls and partitions, shuttering and reinforcing reinforced-concrete elements, pouring foundations, columns, beams and slabs, and applying renders and screeds. You learn to read a plan, to set out a structure and to check the plumb, level and alignment.

## What you will do in the workshop

The programme includes marking out and setting out, laying brick and block courses, building a wall with its openings, shuttering and pouring a footing and a column, preparing concrete, applying an external render and making a screed. Safety actions on site, the wearing of protective equipment and the handling of materials are built into every exercise.

## Where you will work

The mason works on new-build, renovation and fit-out sites, for a construction company or self-employed. The trade requires good physical fitness, an enjoyment of outdoor work and adherence to safety rules around plant and excavations.
