The CMPJ Littoral Region processes the personal data collected on this site solely to answer information requests and to prepare admission files. The processing complies with the applicable Cameroon legislation, in particular the regulations on cybersecurity and the protection of personal data.

## Data collected

The contact form and the pre-registration form collect the information strictly necessary to process the request: name, contact details and, for the pre-registration, the chosen trade. This data is not published.

## Purpose and retention period

The data is used to process the request and is kept for the time necessary for this purpose, then deleted or archived in accordance with the Centre's management rules. It is not resold to third parties.

## Forms and anti-spam

The forms are protected by internal mechanisms (honeypot field and completion-time check). The site does not set advertising cookies and does not use intrusive third-party tracking services. Any audience measurement, if enabled, is anonymous and respects privacy.

## Your rights

To exercise your rights of access, rectification or erasure, write to the Centre's management using the contact details given on the Contact page.
