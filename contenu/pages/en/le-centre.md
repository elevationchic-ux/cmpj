The Multi-purpose Youth Promotion Centre (CMPJ), Littoral Region, is a public community support facility located in New-Bell, Douala. It operates under the supervision of the Ministry of Youth and Civic Education (MINJEC).

## Mission

The CMPJ network is tasked with the social integration of young people and the economic promotion of youth and their groups. It provides local support to the young people of the region in accessing training, employment and income-generating activities.

## Legal framework

The CMPJ were created by Decree No. 2005/151 of 5 May 2005 governing the organisation of MINJEC. Their organisation and operation fall under Decree No. 2010/1099/PM of 7 May 2010, which classifies them into four categories: reference centres, regional centres, departmental centres and subdivision centres. The New-Bell centre is a regional category centre.

## Position within the system

The Centre is part of the CMPJ network coordinated by MINJEC. Like the other centres in the network, it may host units of the National Youth Observatory (ONJ) and take part in the Three-Year Special Plan for Youth (PTS-Youth).

## Administrative organisation

The Centre has a secretariat, which receives admission files, and a finance office, which issues payment receipts. The enrolment steps are described on the Admission page.
