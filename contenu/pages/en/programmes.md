The CMPJ Littoral Region takes part in the national youth programmes led by the Ministry of Youth and Civic Education.

## National Youth Observatory

The CMPJ host units of the National Youth Observatory (ONJ). The ONJ is responsible for observing the situation of young people and providing data useful for the design of youth policies.

## Three-Year Special Plan for Youth

The Centre takes part in the Three-Year Special Plan for Youth (PTS-Youth), the framework within which the training, integration and economic promotion actions carried out by the CMPJ network are organised.

## Partners and agreements

Actions carried out with public, private or non-profit partners are based on official texts and communiqués. The Centre's management specifies their nature and scope when they are published.
