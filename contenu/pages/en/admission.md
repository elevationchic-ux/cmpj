Admission to the CMPJ Littoral Region is by submitting a complete file at the Centre's secretariat. An online pre-registration is offered below to prepare the submission of your file.

## Steps to follow

1. Choose the trade you want and review the corresponding entry requirements.
2. Collect the application file from the secretariat.
3. Prepare the file with the documents required for the current session.
4. Submit the complete file at the secretariat within the session deadlines.
5. Make the required payment at the finance office, which issues a receipt.
6. Follow the instructions given by the secretariat for confirmation of enrolment.

## Documents, fees and calendar

The documents to provide, the tuition fees and the opening and closing dates of enrolment are set for each session by the Centre's management. They are communicated by the secretariat when you collect the file and recalled in the official notices.

## Payment receipt

Every payment results in the issue of a receipt by the Centre's finance office. Keep this receipt, as it confirms your administrative status.
